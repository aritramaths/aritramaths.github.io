About me

I am an Integrated PhD student in Mathematics at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai.

My advisor is Professor Anish Ghosh. My interests lie in Diophantine approximation and homogeneous dynamics, particularly in the use of dynamical methods to study arithmetic problems.

I also enjoy mathematical exposition and teaching. In addition to research-level writing, I like writing expository articles for high-school students and readers interested in mathematics olympiads. This website brings together my research interests, selected notes, and teaching resources.

In my spare time, I enjoy solving problems from past mathematics competitions. I have been interested in astronomy since childhood and enjoy stargazing.

Outside mathematics, I love listening to music and audio stories. I am also interested in history and enjoy reading historical novels.

For academic correspondence, please write to aritra@math.tifr.res.in.

Number theory & dynamics

Research interests

I am interested in the connections between Diophantine approximation, homogeneous dynamics, and ergodic theory.

Diophantine approximation

Rational approximation, continued fractions, and the geometry of numbers.

Homogeneous dynamics

Flows on homogeneous spaces and spaces of lattices, with applications to arithmetic questions.

Ergodic theory

Recurrence, invariant measures, and the statistical behaviour of dynamical systems.

My advisor is Professor Anish Ghosh.
