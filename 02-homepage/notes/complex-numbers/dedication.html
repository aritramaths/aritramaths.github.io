Dedication

To Dr. Ashani Dasgupta

The geometric starting point for these lectures.

I first learned complex numbers from him through spiral similarity. His geometric picture of the subject has stayed with me.

His way of thinking is geometric. My own interests as a number theorist have drawn me toward algebra and analysis. These notes bring those perspectives together: the motions of the plane lead to addition and multiplication, and then to identities, inverses and the field structure familiar from abstract algebra.

I dedicate this work to him with gratitude.

— Aritra Dey
