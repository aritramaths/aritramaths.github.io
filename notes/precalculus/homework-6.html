§ Four practice sittings

Work through Problems 1–4, 5–8, 9–12 and 13–16 in four separate sittings of at most 90 minutes each. For every graph, state its domain and range, label the important points, and mark open endpoints and missing points. When an equation describes a relation, say so and identify every possible output above an allowed input. The buttons lead to the detailed solutions.
