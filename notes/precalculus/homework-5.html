§ Two manageable sittings

Throughout, ⌊x⌋\lfloor x\rfloor means the greatest integer not exceeding xx, and inverse trigonometric values are in radians.

Use the linked definitions, algebra and graph-reading methods in the main lecture. Where a graphical argument needs a later theorem for its full justification, the solution says so. In the solutions, any discussion labelled “After the Calculus module” may be skipped on a first reading.
