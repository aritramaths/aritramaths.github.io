§ Day 3 instructions

Problems 1–15 form the core homework and are designed for one sitting of at most 9090 minutes. Problems 16–25 form an extension sitting of at most 9090 minutes. Determine the domain before the range, show why every claimed endpoint is or is not attained, and do not infer the range from a graph alone. Open “Show solution” below a problem to read its worked solution.

A. Core Range Skills

For Problems 1–15, determine the range. State the domain whenever it is not all of ℝ\mathbb{R}.

§ Checking a range argument

  • State the domain before simplifying.

  • Name every substitution and its attainable interval.

  • Prove the proposed lower and upper bounds.

  • Mark each boundary as attained or merely approached.

  • Check intermediate outputs by finding admissible inputs.

  • Treat floor jumps and excluded denominators separately.
