Inequalities

Methods, proofs and equality cases

Aritra Dey

These lecture notes are intended to develop inequality-solving as a sequence of mathematical decisions rather than as a list of formulas. Each method is introduced through its motivation, hypotheses, proof, equality condition, and carefully chosen applications.
