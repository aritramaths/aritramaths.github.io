The four appendices develop Catalan numbers, equivalence relations and set partitions, integer partitions, and exponential generating functions. They are part of the same book, not additional numbered lectures.

The quick-reference directory of definitions and results links to every numbered theorem and definition in the book. The footer on an ordinary text page takes us back to this map. The table of contents gives the detailed section-by-section route. The notation guide explains the symbols; the alphabetical index locates topics by name.
