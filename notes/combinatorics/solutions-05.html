Results to keep nearby.

Theorem 5.4 (fixed labelled group sizes); Theorem 5.5 (unlabelled groups of prescribed sizes); Theorem 5.6 (Identical Objects into Distinct Boxes); Theorem 5.11 (onto-map formula)

These solutions repeatedly use the central connection of Lecture 5. I want you to say the model aloud before calculating: a distribution of distinct objects into labelled boxes is a map, whereas a distribution of identical objects is an integer-solution problem. Fixed box sizes lead to multinomial coefficients; nonempty labelled boxes lead to onto maps; and unlabelled boxes lead to Stirling numbers or integer partitions.

What to notice.

The words distinct/identical describe the objects, while labelled/unlabelled describe the boxes. Mixing up these two decisions is the most common source of a wrong formula.
